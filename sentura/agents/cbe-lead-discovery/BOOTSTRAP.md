# Cross-Border Lead Discovery — BOOTSTRAP.md

## Enterprise Context

The customer is a cross-border B2B exporter of mobile-phone accessories. Phase 1 focuses on front-end sales research:

- find overseas buyer candidates;
- screen authenticity and risk in a separate employee;
- parse Accio/Alibaba-style chat memory in a separate employee;
- keep all actions inside normal OpenClaw/Sentura employee workspaces and MCP/config wiring.

## Product Scope

Priority categories:

- phone cases: TPU, silicone, leather, rugged, clear, MagSafe;
- screen protectors: tempered glass, privacy glass, hydrogel film;
- chargers: USB-C charger, GaN charger, wall charger, travel adapter;
- cables: USB-C, Lightning, braided, fast charging;
- mobile holders: car mount, magnetic holder, desk stand;
- power accessories: power bank, wireless charger, MagSafe charger;
- repair accessories: LCD screen replacement, battery replacement, repair tool kits.

## Buyer Personas

Target importer, distributor, wholesaler, retailer, repair chain, e-commerce seller, marketplace seller, electronics store, and mobile shop chain.

## Phase-1 Geographies

Default coverage: US, GB, DE, FR, ES, IT, BR, MX, CL, CO.

## Exclusions

Supplier marketplaces and consumer-only commerce sites are excluded by default:

- amazon.com
- alibaba.com
- aliexpress.com
- temu.com
- ebay.com

The operator may add more exclusions.

## Data Classification

- Public company pages and public search snippets are T0/T1.
- Contact candidates and lead packages are T2.
- Raw Accio chat and buyer background reports are T2/T3 and belong to the Accio Memory or Screening employee.
- External channels carry only T0/T1 summaries unless a human approves otherwise.

## Current Milestone Boundary

Milestone 4 implements this Marketing Acquisition employee and the lead-discovery demo route. Screening and Accio Memory employees are separate milestones. In this milestone, handoff means creating a typed payload for the future Screening employee or dashboard route.
