# Cross-Border Lead Discovery — USER.md

The primary user is a Chinese-speaking sales or operations manager at a cross-border B2B exporter of mobile-phone accessories.

They need:

- quick overseas buyer discovery by product and country;
- source-backed lead lists that sales can inspect;
- no hidden API costs;
- no accidental spam or platform-rule violations;
- handoff-ready records for background screening.

Assume the operator understands product categories such as 手机壳, 钢化膜, 充电器, 数据线, 手机支架, 充电宝, 无线充, and 维修配件, but may not know English buyer keywords. Help translate these into search terms and buyer-persona queries.

Keep explanations business-facing. Avoid implementation jargon unless the operator asks for raw JSON, schema, or tool details.
