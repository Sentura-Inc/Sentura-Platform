# Outreach Crafting

## When to Use

When you need to write a personalized outreach message (email, LinkedIn message, or other channel) to a qualified prospect.

## Procedure

1. **Review prospect research** — Read the prospect's CRM record and research notes:
   - Personalization hooks (specific findings about their company/role)
   - Qualification score and ICP match criteria
   - Any previous interactions or touchpoints

2. **Select outreach channel** based on cadence stage:
   - Day 1: Email + LinkedIn connection
   - Day 3: Follow-up email
   - Day 7: Phone/voice or LinkedIn message
   - Day 10: Final email

3. **Craft the message** following these principles:
   - **Subject line** (email): Specific, relevant, no clickbait. Reference something real about their company.
   - **Opening** (1 sentence): Show you know who they are. Reference a trigger event, article, or company initiative.
   - **Value proposition** (1-2 sentences): Connect their pain point to your product's value. Be specific, not generic.
   - **Ask** (1 sentence): Clear, low-commitment CTA. "Would a 15-minute call this week make sense?" not "Buy our product!"
   - **Signature**: Your name, title, company. Keep it clean.

4. **Quality check** before sending:
   - Is it personalized? (Would this only make sense for THIS prospect?)
   - Is it under 100 words? (Short messages get higher response rates)
   - Does it lead with value, not features?
   - Does it have a clear, single CTA?
   - Does it comply with `AGENTS.md § Hard Rules`? (no false claims, no deception, opt-out included)

5. **Send via email MCP or messaging tool**

6. **Log the outreach** in CRM MCP:
   - Record message sent, channel, timestamp
   - Set follow-up reminder based on cadence
   - Update lead status to "contacted"

## Tools Needed

- CRM MCP server — prospect data, activity logging
- Email MCP server — send personalized emails
- `message` — notify sales team of outreach activity

## Safety

- NEVER send generic, non-personalized outreach
- NEVER exceed the daily outreach limit
- NEVER contact opted-out prospects
- NEVER make claims about the product that aren't verified
- ALWAYS include an opt-out/unsubscribe mechanism in emails
- ALWAYS log every outreach action in the CRM
