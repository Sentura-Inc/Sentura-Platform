# Prospect Research

## When to Use

When you receive a new lead, are assigned a target account, or need to research a prospect before outreach.

## Procedure

1. **Identify the prospect** — Get company name and key contact info from CRM MCP or assignment

2. **Company research** using `web_search` and enrichment MCP:
   - Company size, industry, revenue (public data)
   - Recent news, funding rounds, leadership changes
   - Technology stack (check job postings, tech stack databases)
   - Competitors and market position
   - Pain points that align with our ICP

3. **Contact research**:
   - Title, role, reporting structure
   - LinkedIn profile (public info only)
   - Published articles, conference talks, social media activity
   - Mutual connections or shared interests

4. **Qualification check** against ICP:
   - Company size: within target range?
   - Industry: in target verticals?
   - Contact title: decision-maker or influencer?
   - Tech indicators: using complementary/competing solutions?
   - Pain points: match our value proposition?

5. **Score the lead** — Assign qualification score:
   - A (strong match, high intent signals) → prioritize immediately
   - B (good match, no intent signals) → standard outreach cadence
   - C (partial match) → nurture list
   - D (poor match) → disqualify with reason

6. **Log research** in CRM MCP:
   - Update contact and company records
   - Add research notes and qualification score
   - Tag with relevant ICP criteria

7. **Prepare personalization hooks** for outreach:
   - 2-3 specific findings that show you did your homework
   - A relevant pain point tied to your product's value
   - A trigger event (funding, hiring, new product launch) if available

## Tools Needed

- `web_search` — company and contact research
- `web_fetch` — read company websites, press releases, blog posts
- CRM MCP server — lead/contact data, activity logging
- Enrichment MCP server — company data, tech stack, funding info

## Safety

- NEVER scrape private social media profiles or gated content
- NEVER store personal data outside the CRM system
- NEVER fabricate research findings — be honest about what you found
- ALWAYS note the source of each research finding
- ALWAYS check the do-not-contact list before proceeding with outreach
