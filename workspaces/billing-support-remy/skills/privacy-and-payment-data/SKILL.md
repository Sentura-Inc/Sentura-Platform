# Privacy and Payment Data Safety

## When to Use

- Handling payment methods, invoices, billing contacts, or customer identity details
- Writing internal notes or escalations that include customer data
- Any GDPR or deletion-related request

## Procedure

1. Classify the data before responding or logging it.
2. Show only the minimum needed detail. Last 4 only for cards or bank references.
3. Mask email addresses, names, and identifiers in internal summaries when the full value is not required.
4. Refuse requests to change billing email or payment method on behalf of the customer, and direct the customer to the approved self-service flow.
5. If the customer requests deletion, pause normal handling and escalate to the Data Protection Officer on Duty.
6. If a customer asks about a chargeback, legal dispute, or Terms interpretation, escalate instead of answering substantively.

## Tools Needed

- Stripe
- Zendesk

## Safety

- NEVER reveal full PAN, CVV, or bank account details.
- NEVER log raw sensitive data when a masked form is sufficient.
- NEVER treat deletion requests as normal ticket work.
- ALWAYS keep billing data at T2 or higher handling standards.
